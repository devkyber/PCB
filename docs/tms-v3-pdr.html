01System Overview & Component Summary

TMS V3 integrates sensors, ADCs, MCU, wireless telemetry, SD logging, and ignition onto a single PCB. Previous versions used breakout modules on a carrier board; V3 brings the critical analog front-end on-board as bare SMT ICs for better signal integrity and reliability. XBee and MCU remain as socketed modules.

02Signal Chain — Load Cell

Buffer ON vs. Buffer OFF — Critical Design Choice

Critical — Buffer ON Input Range Limitation
With buffer ON, the ADS1256 absolute input range is AGND to AVDD−2.0V. At AVDD=5V this means 0V to 3.0V. The load cell common-mode sits at 2.5V, leaving only 0.5V headroom to the upper limit. Any excitation overshoot, cable-induced offset, or bridge asymmetry could push the input past 3.0V and cause clipping.

Source: ADS1256 datasheet SBAS288, Table 15 — Analog Input: Buffer ON range = AGND to AVDD−2.0V
ParameterBuffer ONBuffer OFFWinner
Abs. input range (AVDD=5V) 0V to 3.0V −0.1V to 5.1V Buffer OFF
CM headroom (2.5V CM) 0.5V margin 2.6V margin Buffer OFF
Noise @ PGA=64, 1000 SPS 0.486 μVRMS 0.365 μVRMS Buffer OFF
Noise-free bits @ PGA=64, 1000 SPS 15.6 bits 16.1 bits Buffer OFF
Input impedance ≥80 MΩ (buffered) ~few MΩ (switched-cap) Buffer ON
Source impedance tolerance High-Z sources OK Source <1 kΩ recommended Buffer ON
AVDD current @ PGA=64 36–50 mA ~10 mA Buffer OFF
Recommendation — Use Buffer OFF
For this application, buffer OFF wins on every metric except input impedance. The Wheatstone bridge output impedance is ~350 Ω — well below the ~1 kΩ maximum for reliable buffer-OFF operation. Buffer OFF gives lower noise (0.365 vs 0.486 μVRMS), wider input range (full rail-to-rail vs. 3.0V ceiling), and lower power (10 mA vs 36–50 mA).

Source: ADS1256 datasheet SBAS288, Tables 1 & 4 (noise), Table 15 (input range), Electrical Characteristics (supply current)

At PGA=64, 1000 SPS, buffer OFF, the input-referred noise is 0.365 μVRMS. For a 100 kg load cell with 10 mV full-scale output at 5V excitation:

MetricValue

Assessment
3.65 g RMS noise at 1 kHz on a 100 kg cell is excellent for motor thrust measurement. The 5V excitation halves the signal compared to 10V but the ADS1256's low noise compensates. If higher SNR is needed later, increasing excitation to 10V doubles the signal with no ADC changes.

Caution — Excitation Stability
The load cell output is ratiometric to excitation voltage. Using the ADS1256 internal 2.5V reference (non-ratiometric) means LM7805 output noise and ripple appear as measurement error. Consider: (a) using the excitation voltage as the ADC reference via a precision resistive divider (ratiometric — cancels excitation noise), or (b) adding LC filtering on the excitation rail.

05Power Architecture

Separate Igniter Battery — Design Rationale
Using a dedicated battery for the igniter provides complete electrical isolation between measurement and ignition. The igniter draws 1–3A pulses that would cause ground bounce and supply droops if shared with the ADC rail. With separate batteries, a short or igniter fault cannot brown-out the data acquisition system. The igniter circuit is simple: battery → physical ARM key switch → N-channel MOSFET (GPIO-controlled) → e-match.

3.3V Regulator — Revised Assessment

XBee Current Corrected — Teensy LDO Sufficient
The XBee 3 standard module (non-PRO) draws 40 mA during TX and 15 mA during RX — not the 100+ mA previously estimated (that figure applies to the XBee 3 PRO variant at max TX power). The Teensy 4.1 onboard 3.3V LDO (250 mA capacity) has adequate margin:

Teensy core ~100 mA + XBee TX 40 mA + ADS1256 DVDD 2.5 mA + ADS1220 DVDD 0.3 mA + SD card ~20 mA ≈ 163 mA peak

This is 65% of the 250 mA rating. A dedicated 3.3V LDO is no longer required.

Source: XBee 3 802.15.4 datasheet (Digi), Table "DC Characteristics" — ITX=40 mA @ +5 dBm, IRX=15 mA

07Ignition Circuit

The ignition system uses a separate battery. The circuit is the simplest possible: battery → physical ARM switch → MOSFET switch → e-match.

Igniter Components

ComponentPurposeNotes
XT60U-F (2nd connector) Igniter battery input Separate from electronics battery. Clear silkscreen label.
Key switch / covered toggle Physical ARM inhibit Physically disconnects igniter power. Prevents accidental arming.
N-channel MOSFET (logic-level) FIRE switch RDS(on) < 50 mΩ, VGS(th) < 2V for 3.3V drive. e.g., AO3400A (SOT-23, SMT).
10 kΩ pull-down on gate Default-off safety Ensures FET stays OFF during Teensy boot when GPIO floats.
Screw terminal (2-pos) E-match connection Field-serviceable. Clearly labeled polarity.
Caution — GPIO Pull-down is Essential
During Teensy boot, GPIO pins float for ~100 ms before firmware initializes them. Without the 10 kΩ pull-down, the FET may conduct and fire the igniter during power-on. Non-negotiable safety hardware.

08Design Findings

Findings from preliminary review, sorted by severity. Rev B updates marked.

F-01 — ADS1256 Buffer ON limits input range to 3.0V (AVDD−2V)
With buffer ON and AVDD=5V, the absolute input range is 0V to 3.0V. Load cell CM at 2.5V has only 0.5V headroom. Buffer OFF is mandatory — also gives lower noise (0.365 vs 0.486 μVRMS) and lower power (10 vs 36–50 mA).
Source: ADS1256 SBAS288 Table 15 • Status: RESOLVED — use buffer OFF
F-02 — Pressure sensor 5V output exceeds 3.3V ADC input range
ADS1220 at 3.3V AVDD: absolute max input = 3.6V. IPSU outputs 0–5V. Must power ADS1220 AVDD at 5V (split supply with DVDD=3.3V).
Source: ADS1220 SBAS501D absolute max ratings • Status: RESOLVED — ADS1220IPWR with AVDD=5V, DVDD=3.3V
F-03 — 3S LiPo margins insufficient
3S at low SOC delivers 9.0V — marginal for pressure sensor (min 9V) and insufficient for LM7810 (min 12V). With 4S, LM7810 is eliminated (pressure sensor fed directly from VBAT 12–16.8V).
Status: RESOLVED — use 4S, remove LM7810
F-04 — Teensy 4.1 is NOT 5V-tolerant [NEW Rev B]
NXP iMXRT1062 GPIOs are 3.3V only. If DVDD is accidentally connected to 5V, all SPI lines are at risk. Verify all signal level domains during schematic review.
Status: OPEN — verify in schematic
F-05 — Load cell excitation noise affects measurement (non-ratiometric)
Using ADS1256 internal 2.5V reference means LM7805 noise appears in measurement. Consider ratiometric reference (derive VREF from excitation via divider).
Status: OPEN — design decision D-04
F-06 — No input protection on ADC analog inputs
No TVS diodes or series resistors for ESD/transient protection. Load cell cables are several metres long. Add series 100 Ω + TVS clamp (e.g., PESD5V0S2BT).
Status: OPEN — add to schematic
F-07 — SPI bus contention risk
ADS1256 + ADS1220 should be on separate SPI buses. Teensy 4.1 has SPI, SPI1, SPI2.
Status: RESOLVED — ADS1256 on SPI0, ADS1220 on SPI1
F-08 — GPIO pull-down on igniter MOSFET gate is safety-critical
During Teensy boot (~100 ms) GPIO pins float. Without 10 kΩ pull-down on FET gate, igniter may fire on power-up.
Status: OPEN — add to schematic
F-09 — Missing decoupling capacitors
Each ADC needs 100 nF + 10 μF at AVDD and DVDD. LM7805 needs input/output caps (10 μF min). With buffer OFF, switched-cap input generates AVDD current spikes — local decoupling critical for noise performance.
Status: OPEN — add to BOM and schematic
F-10 — No test points defined
Add labeled test points: VBAT, 5V, 3.3V, ADC AIN+/AIN−, DRDY, igniter output.
Status: OPEN
F-11 — LED status indicators not specified
Add LEDs: power (green), logging (blue), ARM (red), telemetry (amber).
Status: OPEN
